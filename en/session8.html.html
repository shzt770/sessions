Session 8 · Slide 3

The Market Has
Crashed Before. It Always Came Back.

Every single time the market collapsed, people were convinced it was different this time. It never was.

1929 — The Great Depression Market recovered within years
1987 — Black Monday (down 22% in one day) Recovered within two years
2000 — Dot-com bubble burst New highs within 7 years
2008 — Financial crisis Market doubled within 5 years
2020 — COVID crash (down 34% in one month) New all-time high within 6 months
✦ People who stayed in the market made money. People who panicked and sold didn't.
Session 8 · Slide 4

The Jargon
Decoded

The terminology is designed to sound complicated. It isn't. Here's what it actually means.

Stock A small ownership stake in a company — you're a shareholder, not a lender
Bond A loan you give to a government or company — they pay you back with interest
Index A basket of companies — the S&P 500 is simply the 500 largest companies in America
ETF A fund that buys the whole index in one click — instant diversification, minimal fees
