Session 9 · Slide 3

Roth IRA vs.
Traditional IRA

One question decides it: would you rather pay tax on the seed — or on the harvest?

Traditional IRA

  • Contribute pre-tax dollars
  • Save on taxes now
  • Pay taxes when you withdraw
  • Best if your income is high today
⟷

Roth IRA

  • Contribute after-tax dollars
  • Pay taxes now
  • Withdrawals in retirement are tax-free
  • Best if your income is lower today
✦ For most of our clients — Roth IRA wins. Your money grows for 30 years completely tax-free.
