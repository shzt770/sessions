Find the Hidden Money

Three concrete actions — each one could put real money back in your pocket.

Play with the compound interest calculator

Check your employee benefits
Contact HR and ask: Do we have a 401(k) match? An HSA? Stock options? Any benefit I haven't enrolled in yet?
